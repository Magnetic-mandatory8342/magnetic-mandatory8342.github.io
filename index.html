🚀 Using the App Day to Day

There is almost nothing to learn. The app handles the timing on its own. Here is the whole routine:

  1. Click the eye icon in the menu bar to see the current status and how long is left before your next break.
  2. Keep working normally. When 20 minutes pass, the screen dims to signal that it is time to look away.
  3. Look at something far away for about 20 seconds. A window, a wall, a plant, anything in the distance.
  4. The screen returns to normal and the next 20-minute cycle begins automatically.

You can pause reminders during a presentation, a call, or a video, and turn them back on when you are ready. Everything is controlled from the same menu bar icon.

💡 Why the 20-20-20 Rule Helps

When you focus on a screen, you blink less often and your eyes stay locked at one distance for a long stretch. That is what causes the dry, heavy, headache-adjacent feeling known as digital eye strain, or computer vision syndrome.

Short, regular breaks are far easier to keep than one long rest at the end of the day. Taking a 20-second pause every 20 minutes lets the muscles that focus your eyes relax and gives your blinking a chance to reset. Most people notice the difference within a day or two of steady use.
